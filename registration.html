Competition

Sugama Sangeeta Gayana Spardhe (ಸುಗಮ ಸಂಗೀತ ಗಾಯನ ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆ) — a melody singing competition celebrating Karnataka's rich Sugama Sangeetha tradition.

📅

Event Dates

Main event: 12 & 13 December 2026.
Competition round may be scheduled on 11 December 2026 at 9:00 AM. Exact schedule will be communicated to registered participants.

📍

Venue

Sri ChannaBasava PattaDevaR Rang Mandir, Bidar, Karnataka 585401

👤

Eligibility

Open to participants aged 16 to 20 years. Must be a resident of Karnataka or of Kannada origin.

💰

Registration Fee

Rules & Guidelines

ನಿಯಮಗಳು / Competition Rules

  • 1 Participants must be between 16 and 20 years of age on the date of the competition.
  • 2 Each participant may perform one Sugama Sangeetha (light music) song in Kannada.
  • 3 Participants may bring their own musical instruments or use the instruments provided at the venue.
  • 4 Film songs and folk songs are not permitted. Only Sugama Sangeetha compositions are accepted.
  • 5 Performance time: 5 to 7 minutes per participant.
  • 6 Decision of the judges is final and binding.
  • 7 Registration fee of ₹200 is non-refundable once verified.
  • 8 Participants below 18 years require written consent from a parent or guardian.
  • 9 Participants must carry a valid photo ID and their event pass on the day of the competition.
